01 / THE STORY

What remains
when someone leaves?

After his relationship ends, Aditya must face the friendships he neglected, the interests he abandoned, and the parts of himself he left behind.

Through ordinary mornings, shared cups of chai, difficult conversations, and unexpected beginnings, he slowly rebuilds a life that feels like his own.

The Road Back to Myself is a heartfelt story about love, loss, friendship, and the quiet courage it takes to start over. It's a reminder that sometimes, the end of a chapter is not the end of the story, but the beginning of a more honest and meaningful one.

At the heart of this story is human connection: the people we return to, the parts of ourselves we rediscover, and the quiet possibility of beginning again.
